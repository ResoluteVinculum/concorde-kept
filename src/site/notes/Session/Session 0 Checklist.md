> [!note] A Balanced Party
> 
> The classic D&D party comprises a [[Rules/Source/classes/cleric-xphb\|Cleric]], [[Rules/Source/classes/fighter-xphb\|Fighter]], [[Rules/Source/classes/rogue-xphb\|Rogue]], and [[Rules/Source/classes/wizard-xphb\|Wizard]]. Those four classes have the longest history in the game, but more importantly, they bring a balanced mix of capabilities to adventures. You're welcome to use that party setup or modify it using these guidelines:
> 
> - **Cleric.** Replace with [[Rules/Source/classes/bard-xphb\|Bard]] or [[Rules/Source/classes/druid-xphb\|Druid]]  
> - **Fighter.** Replace with [[Rules/Source/classes/barbarian-xphb\|Barbarian]], [[Rules/Source/classes/monk-xphb\|Monk]], [[Rules/Source/classes/paladin-xphb\|Paladin]], or [[Rules/Source/classes/ranger-xphb\|Ranger]]  
> - **Rogue.** Replace with [[Rules/Source/classes/bard-xphb\|Bard]] or [[Rules/Source/classes/ranger-xphb\|Ranger]]  
> - **Wizard.** Replace with [[Rules/Source/classes/bard-xphb\|Bard]], [[Rules/Source/classes/sorcerer-xphb\|Sorcerer]], or [[Rules/Source/classes/warlock-xphb\|Warlock]]
