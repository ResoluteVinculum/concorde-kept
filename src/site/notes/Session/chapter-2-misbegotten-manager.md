### Guardian
The players will be guided down the various roads to ALL three destinations.

The first stop they make will involve an encounter with rats and a single mud mephit
```encounter
name: Muddy Mice
creatures:
  - 4:Rat
  - 2:Giant Rat (Diseased)
  - 1:Mud Mephit (XMM)
party: Workers
```


The third stop and throughout will have some clues about the world.
1. Town noticeboard shows decrees from the [[Story/Setting/Triskelion\|Triskelion]] completely overtaken by vines and bark, except for those displaying the individual seals of the three members
2. Roots on government buildings are not as salubrious, they appear diseased, specifically at the property boundaries
3. If they players go to any of the boundaries of the town (founts), the players will find perfect coils of vines, sitting as obelisks. These obelisks are completely immune to any damage and when attacked emit a lightly [[Rules/Source/traps-hazards/poisonous-gas-xdmg\|Poisonous Gas]] 
4. A statue outside of the city market has been overtaken by thick, wooded vines/roots that give the statue a more reptilian look. What was once a expertly carved cape now looks like the frills of a cobra.
5. Upon close inspection `DC 15 Investigation` , the vines don't look entirely natural - concentric circles, sometimes perfectly straight, even the bark appears patterned.
6. Leaves and moss grow in scaled patterns, the cells of leaves aren't *square*, the moss grows in the concentric, layered pattern, like a reptiles abdomen.
7. The gargoyles and lion statues once adorning the city hall now resemble a dragon
8. All of the creatures the players fight speak draconic to one another.

## Puzzle/RP
Getting to the center of town without having interrogated the different POIs about town and finding the 6 total artefacts about town.
- 2 Intricately Carved wooden totems, each missing two obvious pieces. Visible by a pit and clear inlay pattern, the minor effects they create are reflected in the gemstones and the inlays
	- One depicts a flame with missing green gemstone - Creates a slight irritability
	- One depicts a holy symbol centered around a pit that once housed a black gemstone - Elevated senses, slightly overwhelming
- 2 Stones that each are bonded to some small woodchips
	- Green Stone - Emotionally dulled, emotions come a little late and dampened
	- Black Stone - Numbed senses, hearing/touch specifically. 
- 2 Inlays
	- Metallic Frame of Fire - Warm to the touch, makes you uncomfortably warm which eases when near the totem
	- Metallic Leaf inlay - Greens are more vibrant, stone colors seem more muted.

The magical effects are entirely negated when each complete totem is assembled. The players will also become immune to the effects of the town while the spell is active and automatically succeed on the check to enter the well. The holder of each totem will receive a +2 to `WIS` tests and +2 to `STR` tests while attuned. One must assemble the totem to gain attunement, and cannot be the one who disassembled it.

The players will encounter various fey around town, both friendly and not, but nothing large.

## Trick or Setback
When trying to enter the well at the center of town:
- With no Pieces: `DC 20+ WIS Save` to not be pushed back to the noticeboard.
- With Pieces:
	- 1-2: The vines have locked completely around the well opening. Any attempt at entry receives the effects of the [[Rules/Source/spells/poison-spray-xphb\|Poison Spray]] spell where the room will slowly fill with the gas. Being affected by the gas immediately triggers the transition to the noticeboard.
	- 3-5: The players are thrust into combat with each other, revelations of who/what they're fighting, when the illusion ends, they are pushed back to the noticeboard, but will remember the voice of the hag casting the incantation, but will still face a `DC 15 WIS Save` to enter the well (hope everyone makes it)

## Climax
In the concentration ring of the city (cave), the players will begin to feel the effects of a [[Rules/Source/spells/geas-xphb\|Geas]] spell. It begins with the effects of [[Rules/Source/spells/suggestion-xphb\|Suggestion]] and progresses to at the same rate as the town's effects. The player's are able to interrogate the voice influencing their actions (if they ask questions, they'll get manipulation in response). They must interrogate this voice to make progress.

The players will make further mental saving throws, increasing as the fight goes on. On failures, they will be thrown into their combative memories and must unwittingly fight each other.

**==It would be good to end the session on the initiative roll after describing some of them not able to see the unaffected party==**
```encounter
name: Trickster's Effigy
creatures:
  - Arch-hag (XMM)
  - 2:Worg
party: Workers
```

The players can work to split the attention of the psychic force keeping them from approaching/attacking the effigy.
