# Death at Sunset - Use the Book
## Story Hook
This is a group of adventurers who all followed the heroic call to Redwood Watch to solve the issues they've been having for a few years. Many adventurers have come and gone over the course of the last nine years. The town does not produce anything for the [[Story/Setting/Embren\|Duchy]], so the process of getting government sanctioned heroes to help is long and ceaseless. The players will be able to rally the existing townsfolk into the fight.

## Notes
The players fail to slay [[Characters/NPC/Eliana Verdon\|Klaeryll]] here. (750 years old at the time and recently taken over the dead dragon's layer.)
