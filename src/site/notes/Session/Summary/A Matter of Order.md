# Contract
The party arrived at [[Story/Setting/Castle Embrea\|Castle Embrea]] and met both the [[Characters/NPC/Duke Holun Blither\|Duke]] and the [[Story/Setting/Triskelion\|Triskelion]]. [[Characters/PC/Benji Pickletoes\|Benji Pickletoes]] insulted [[Story/Setting/Embren\|Embren]]'s soldiers before [[Characters/NPC/Eliana Verdon\|Eliana Verdon]] interrupted the conversation to present the first job:  [[Session/chapter-1-matter-of-order\|A Matter of Order]].

# Arrival and Scrutiny
The party headed to [[Story/Setting/Hargen\|Hargen]] and were met with slamming shutters and locked doors. [[Characters/PC/Flop\|Flop]] scared the town drunk and learned that "grain" is important to [[Story/Setting/Hargen\|Hargen]]. Filled with righteous motivations: taxes and intimidation, the party headed straight to the city hall where they intimidated the secretary into a tense meeting with [[Characters/NPC/Osonia\|Mayor Osonia]] that quickly devolved into [[Characters/PC/Sprocket\|Sprocket]] throwing the woman over his shoulder and [[Characters/PC/Flop\|Flop]] tying up the secretary. The party removed both city employees from the town hall after discovering that there is a monstrous threat in the mill stagnating the town economy.

# Protectors
Making their way to the mill, the party encountered the self appointed protectors of the town. The denizens of Hargen expressed the unwanted attention from the [[Story/Setting/Triskelion\|Triskelion]], having the mayor over their shoulder and another citizen being led like a prisoner, there was no recourse. The protectors began fighting, sternly stating "You will leave." [[Story/Setting/Hargen\|Hargen]] quickly learned the power of magic as swords and maces were swung to little avail and [[Characters/PC/Val Verhen\|Val Verhen]] thunderous power launched one of the toughs across the road through a door, after which [[Characters/PC/Sprocket\|Sprocket]] killed the townsmen inciting the fight. The last [[Rules/Source/bestiary/humanoid/tough-xmm\|Tough]] tried to surrender, but the party too knocked him unconcious.

# Disguised Depository
Before entering the mill the agents of the [[Story/Setting/Triskelion\|Triskelion]] negotiated through the Mayor's fear of the mill and brought her in with them. Inside, they discovered a corpse at in the workshop. Adhered to the remnants of a chest with a crushed torso, the corpse was patiently watching over a magical broom and two potions of healing. Finding bite marks in the worktables, the tax collectors made their way upstairs to the mechanism. Met with a dust covered room full of containers. A chest was thrown down the stairs, breaking many of them and restricting the only way out of the mill. [[Characters/PC/Val Verhen\|Val Verhen]] used mage hand to investigate a journal on the center table, safely revealing a juvenile mimic, which [[Characters/PC/Benji Pickletoes\|Benji Pickletoes]] quickly dispatched. Angered by the child's death, the chest mimic began its assault. [[Characters/PC/Flop\|Flip]] flopped down the stairs, breaking another two, and utilized his brand new broom to make the jump back up. After easily killing the mimic, Osonia bestowed a reward upon the party, in spite of a citizen's death, which [[Characters/PC/Benji Pickletoes\|Benji Pickletoes]] seemed duty bound to use as payment for the lapse in taxes.

In the midst of a set of gnarled, rotting vines, the party observed a sending stone identical to their own emit a single word, "*Don't*," before shattering into dust. Near the stone, the party was able to find
- 1 Scroll of [[Rules/Source/spells/jump-xphb\|Jump]]
- 1 Vial [[Rules/Source/items/dust-of-disappearance-xdmg\|Dust of Disappearance]]
- 1 [[Rules/Source/items/cloak-of-billowing-xdmg\|Cloak of Billowing]]
- 1 [[Rules/Source/items/dread-helm-xdmg\|Dread Helm]]
- 1 [[Rules/Source/items/sword-of-vengeance-xdmg\|Shortsword of Vengeance]]
- 1 Mortar and Pestle that extinguishes non-magical flames in a 20-foot radius
all of which were coated in a green ooze that seemed to protect the items from deterioration.
