# Boon of Fate
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 210. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Improve Fate.** When you or another creature within 60 feet of you succeeds on or fails a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/d20-test-xphb\|D20 Test]], you can roll `2d4` and apply the total rolled as a bonus or penalty to the `d20` roll. Once you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you roll [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/initiative-xphb\|Initiative]] or finish a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/short-rest-xphb\|Short]] or [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/long-rest-xphb\|Long Rest]].
