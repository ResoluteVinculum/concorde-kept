# Alert
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 200. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Initiative Proficiency.** When you roll [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/initiative-xphb\|Initiative]], you can add your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/proficiency-xphb\|Proficiency Bonus]] to the roll.

**Initiative Swap.** Immediately after you roll [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/initiative-xphb\|Initiative]], you can swap your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/initiative-xphb\|Initiative]] with the [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/initiative-xphb\|Initiative]] of one willing ally in the same combat. You can't make this swap if you or the ally has the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition.
