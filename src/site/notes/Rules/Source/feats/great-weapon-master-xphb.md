# Great Weapon Master
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 204*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th; Strength 13 or higher

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Heavy Weapon Mastery.** When you hit a creature with a weapon that has the [[Rules/Source/item-properties#Heavy\|Heavy]] property as part of the [[Rules/Source/actions#Attack\|Attack]] action on your turn, you can cause the weapon to deal extra damage to the target. The extra damage equals your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/proficiency-xphb\|Proficiency Bonus]].

**Hew.** Immediately after you score a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/critical-hit-xphb\|Critical Hit]] with a Melee weapon or reduce a creature to 0 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]] with one, you can make one attack with the same weapon as a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb\|Bonus Action]].
