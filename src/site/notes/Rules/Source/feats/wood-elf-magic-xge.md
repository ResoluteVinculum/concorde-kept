# Wood Elf Magic
*Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything p. 75*  

**Prerequisite**: [[Rules/Source/races/elf-xphb\|Elf]]

You learn the magic of the primeval woods, which are revered and protected by your people. You learn one druid cantrip of your choice. You also learn the [[Rules/Source/spells/longstrider-xphb\|longstrider]] and [[Rules/Source/spells/pass-without-trace-xphb\|pass without trace]] spells, each of which you can cast once without expending a spell slot. You regain the ability to cast these two spells in this way when you finish a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for all three spells.
