# Acolyte
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 178. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*  
![/Rules/Source/backgrounds/img/acolyte.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/backgrounds/img/acolyte.webp)

- **Ability Scores.** Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma  
- **Feat.** [[Rules/Source/feats/magic-initiate-xphb\|Magic Initiate]] (Cleric)  
- **Skill Proficiencies.** [[Rules/Source/skills#Insight\|Insight]], [[Rules/Source/skills#Religion\|Religion]]  
- **Tool Proficiency.** [[Rules/Source/items/calligraphers-supplies-xphb\|Calligrapher's Supplies]]  
- **Equipment.** Choose A or B: (A) [[Rules/Source/items/calligraphers-supplies-xphb\|Calligrapher's Supplies]], [[Rules/Source/items/book-xphb\|Book (prayers)]], [[Rules/Source/items/holy-symbol-xphb\|Holy Symbol]], [[Rules/Source/items/parchment-xphb\|Parchment]] (10 sheets), [[Rules/Source/items/robe-xphb\|Robe]], 8 GP; or (B) 50 GP  

You devoted yourself to service in a temple, either nestled in a town or secluded in a sacred grove. There you performed rites in honor of a god or pantheon. You served under a priest and studied religion. Thanks to your priest's instruction and your own devotion, you also learned how to channel a modicum of divine power in service to your place of worship and the people who prayed there.
