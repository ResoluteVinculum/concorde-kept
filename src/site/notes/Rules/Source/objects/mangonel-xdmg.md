# Mangonel

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 97*  

A Mangonel is a catapult that hurls heavy projectiles in a high arc, so it can hit targets behind walls. Loading a Mangonel requires two [[Rules/Source/actions#Utilize\|Utilize]] actions, and aiming it requires two more [[Rules/Source/actions#Utilize\|Utilize]] actions. Then a crew member can take the Mangonel Stone action.
