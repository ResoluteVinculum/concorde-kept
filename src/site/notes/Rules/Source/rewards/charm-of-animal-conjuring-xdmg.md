# Charm of Animal Conjuring

This Charm allows you to cast [[Rules/Source/spells/conjure-animals-xphb\|Conjure Animals]]. Once used three times, the Charm vanishes from you.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 99*
