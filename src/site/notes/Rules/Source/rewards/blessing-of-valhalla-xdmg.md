# Blessing of Valhalla

This Blessing grants you the power to summon spirit warriors, as if you are blowing a [[Rules/Source/items/horn-of-valhalla-silver-xdmg\|silver Horn of Valhalla]]. Once you use this Blessing, you can't use it again until 7 days have passed.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 99*
