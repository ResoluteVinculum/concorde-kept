# Charm of the Slayer

One weapon in your possession becomes a [[Rules/Source/items/dragon-slayer-xdmg\|Dragon Slayer]] or [[Rules/Source/items/giant-slayer-xdmg\|Giant Slayer]] (DM's choice) for the next 9 days. The Charm then vanishes from you, and the weapon returns to normal.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 99*
