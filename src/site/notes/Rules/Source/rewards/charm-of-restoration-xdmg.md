# Charm of Restoration

This Charm has 3 charges. You can expend some of its charges to cast one of the following spells: [[Rules/Source/spells/greater-restoration-xphb\|Greater Restoration]] (2 charges) or [[Rules/Source/spells/lesser-restoration-xphb\|Lesser Restoration]] (1 charge). Once all its charges have been expended, the Charm vanishes from you.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 99*
