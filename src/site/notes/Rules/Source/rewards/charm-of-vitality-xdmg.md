# Charm of Vitality

This Charm allows you to give yourself the benefit of a [[Rules/Source/items/potion-of-vitality-xdmg\|Potion of Vitality]] as a [[Rules/Source/actions#Magic\|Magic]] action. Once you do so, the Charm vanishes from you.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 99*
