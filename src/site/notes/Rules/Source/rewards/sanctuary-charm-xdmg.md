# Sanctuary Charm
*Charm*  

After spending a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/long-rest-xphb\|Long Rest]] in your "Bastion", you gain a magical Charm (see ""Supernatural Gifts"" in "chapter 3") that lasts for 7 days or until you use it. The Charm allows you to cast [[Rules/Source/spells/healing-word-xphb\|Healing Word]] once without expending a spell slot. You can't gain this Charm again while you still have it.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 345*
