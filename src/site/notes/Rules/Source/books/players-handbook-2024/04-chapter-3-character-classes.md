# Chapter 3: Character Classes
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024), p. 49* 

![The heroes Mercion, Molliv...](/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/025-03-050-opening-art.webp#center "The heroes Mercion, Molliver, Strongheart, and Ringlerun protect a portal to another plane from diabolical invaders")

Character class provides a character's most exciting capabilities. This chapter offers twelve classes, each of which contains four subclasses—all summarized below.

![/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/026-03-002-barbarian-symbol.webp\|center wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/026-03-002-barbarian-symbol.webp)

## Barbarian

Storm with Rage, and wade into hand-to-hand combat. Then follow the Path of the...

- [[Rules/Source/classes/barbarian-xphb-path-of-the-berserker-xphb\|Path of the Berserker]] to unleash raw violence.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/barbarian-xphb-path-of-the-wild-heart-xphb\|Path of the Wild Heart]] to manifest kinship with animals.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/barbarian-xphb-path-of-the-world-tree-xphb\|Path of the World Tree]] to tap into cosmic vitality.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/barbarian-xphb-path-of-the-zealot-xphb\|Path of the Zealot]] to rage in union with a god.  

![/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/027-03-003-bard-symbol.webp\|center wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/027-03-003-bard-symbol.webp)

## Bard

Perform spells that inspire and heal allies or beguile foes. Then join the College of...

- [[Rules/Source/classes/bard-xphb-college-of-dance-xphb\|College of Dance]] to harness agility in battle.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/bard-xphb-college-of-glamour-xphb\|College of Glamour]] to weave beguiling Feywild magic.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/bard-xphb-college-of-lore-xphb\|College of Lore]] to collect knowledge and magical secrets.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/bard-xphb-college-of-valor-xphb\|College of Valor]] to wield weapons with spells.  

![/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/028-03-004-cleric-symbol.webp\|center wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/028-03-004-cleric-symbol.webp)

## Cleric

Invoke divine magic to heal, bolster, and smite. Then harness the...

- [[Rules/Source/classes/cleric-xphb-life-domain-xphb\|Life Domain]] to be a master of healing.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/cleric-xphb-light-domain-xphb\|Light Domain]] to wield searing, warding light.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/cleric-xphb-trickery-domain-xphb\|Trickery Domain]] to bedevil foes with mischief.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/cleric-xphb-war-domain-xphb\|War Domain]] to inspire valor and chastise foes.  

![/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/029-03-005-druid-symbol.webp\|center wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/029-03-005-druid-symbol.webp)

## Druid

Channel nature magic to heal, shape-shift, and control the elements. Then join the Circle of the...

- [[Rules/Source/classes/druid-xphb-circle-of-the-land-xphb\|Circle of the Land]] to draw on the magic of the environment.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/druid-xphb-circle-of-the-moon-xphb\|Circle of the Moon]] to adopt powerful animal forms.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/druid-xphb-circle-of-the-sea-xphb\|Circle of the Sea]] to channel tides and storms.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/druid-xphb-circle-of-the-stars-xphb\|Circle of the Stars]] to gain powers in a starry form.  

![/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/030-03-006-fighter-symbol.webp\|center wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/030-03-006-fighter-symbol.webp)

## Fighter

Master all weapons and armor. Then embody the...

- [[Rules/Source/classes/fighter-xphb-battle-master-xphb\|Battle Master]] to use special combat maneuvers.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/fighter-xphb-champion-xphb\|Champion]] to strive for peak combat prowess.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/fighter-xphb-eldritch-knight-xphb\|Eldritch Knight]] to learn spells to aid in combat.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/fighter-xphb-psi-warrior-xphb\|Psi Warrior]] to augment attacks with psionic power.  

![/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/031-03-007-monk-symbol.webp\|center wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/031-03-007-monk-symbol.webp)

## Monk

Dart in and out of melee while striking fast and hard. Then become a Warrior of...

- [[Rules/Source/classes/monk-xphb-warrior-of-mercy-xphb\|Warrior of Mercy]] to heal or harm with a touch.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/monk-xphb-warrior-of-shadow-xphb\|Warrior of Shadow]] to employ shadows for subterfuge.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/monk-xphb-warrior-of-the-elements-xphb\|Warrior of the Elements]] to wield elemental power.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/monk-xphb-warrior-of-the-open-hand-xphb\|Warrior of the Open Hand]] to master unarmed combat.  

![/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/032-03-008-paladin-symbol.webp\|center wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/032-03-008-paladin-symbol.webp)

## Paladin

Smite foes and shield allies with divine and martial might. Then swear the Oath of...

- [[Rules/Source/classes/paladin-xphb-oath-of-devotion-xphb\|Oath of Devotion]] to emulate the angels of justice.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/paladin-xphb-oath-of-glory-xphb\|Oath of Glory]] to reach the heights of heroism.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/paladin-xphb-oath-of-the-ancients-xphb\|Oath of the Ancients]] to preserve life, joy, and nature.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/paladin-xphb-oath-of-vengeance-xphb\|Oath of Vengeance]] to hunt down evildoers.  

![/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/033-03-009-ranger-symbol.webp\|center wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/033-03-009-ranger-symbol.webp)

## Ranger

Weave together martial prowess, nature magic, and survival skills. Then embody the...

- [[Rules/Source/classes/ranger-xphb-beast-master-xphb\|Beast Master]] to bond with a primal beast.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/ranger-xphb-fey-wanderer-xphb\|Fey Wanderer]] to manifest fey mirth and fury.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/ranger-xphb-gloom-stalker-xphb\|Gloom Stalker]] to hunt foes that lurk in darkness.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/ranger-xphb-hunter-xphb\|Hunter]] to protect nature with martial versatility.  

![/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/034-03-010-rogue-symbol.webp\|center wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/034-03-010-rogue-symbol.webp)

## Rogue

Launch deadly Sneak Attacks while avoiding harm through stealth. Then embody the...

- [[Rules/Source/classes/rogue-xphb-arcane-trickster-xphb\|Arcane Trickster]] to enhance stealth with spells.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/rogue-xphb-assassin-xphb\|Assassin]] to deliver ambushes and poison.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/rogue-xphb-soulknife-xphb\|Soulknife]] to strike foes with psi blades.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/rogue-xphb-thief-xphb\|Thief]] to master infiltration and treasure hunting.  

![/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/035-03-011-sorcerer-symbol.webp\|center wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/035-03-011-sorcerer-symbol.webp)

## Sorcerer

Wield magic innate to your being, shaping the power to your will. Then channel...

- [[Rules/Source/classes/sorcerer-xphb-aberrant-sorcery-xphb\|Aberrant Sorcery]] to use strange psionic magic.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/sorcerer-xphb-clockwork-sorcery-xphb\|Clockwork Sorcery]] to harness cosmic forces of order.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/sorcerer-xphb-draconic-sorcery-xphb\|Draconic Sorcery]] to breathe the magic of dragons.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/sorcerer-xphb-wild-magic-sorcery-xphb\|Wild Magic Sorcery]] to unleash chaos magic.  

![/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/036-03-012-warlock-symbol.webp\|center wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/036-03-012-warlock-symbol.webp)

## Warlock

Cast spells derived from occult knowledge. Then form a pact with the...

- [[Rules/Source/classes/warlock-xphb-archfey-patron-xphb\|Archfey Patron]] to teleport and wield fey magic.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/warlock-xphb-celestial-patron-xphb\|Celestial Patron]] to heal with heavenly magic.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/warlock-xphb-fiend-patron-xphb\|Fiend Patron]] to call on sinister powers.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/warlock-xphb-great-old-one-patron-xphb\|Great Old One Patron]] to delve into forbidden lore.  

![/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/037-03-013-wizard-symbol.webp\|center wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/books/players-handbook-2024/img/037-03-013-wizard-symbol.webp)

## Wizard

Study arcane magic and master spells for every purpose. Then embody the...

- [[Rules/Source/classes/wizard-xphb-abjurer-xphb\|Abjurer]] to shield allies and banish foes.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/wizard-xphb-diviner-xphb\|Diviner]] to learn the multiverse's secrets.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/wizard-xphb-evoker-xphb\|Evoker]] to create explosive effects.  
- [[Rules/Source/classes/wizard-xphb-illusionist-xphb\|Illusionist]] to weave spells of deception.
