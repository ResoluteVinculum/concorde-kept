## Bomb

As an action, you can light a Bomb and throw it at a point up to 60 feet away, where it explodes. Each creature in a 5-foot-radius [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/sphere-area-of-effect-xphb\|Sphere]] centered on that point makes a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking `3d6` Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Dynamite Stick

As an action, you can light a Dynamite Stick and throw it at a point up to 60 feet away, where it explodes. Each creature in a 5-foot-radius [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/sphere-area-of-effect-xphb\|Sphere]] centered on that point makes a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking `3d6` Force damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

It takes 1 minute to bind two or more Dynamite Sticks together so they explode at the same time. Each stick after the first increases the damage by `1d6` (to a maximum of `10d6`) and the effect's radius by 5 feet (to a maximum of 20 feet).

It takes 1 minute to rig dynamite with a longer fuse so it explodes after a longer period of time, such as 1 minute or 10 minutes.

## Grenades and Grenade Launchers

As an action, you can either throw a grenade at a point up to 60 feet away or use a Grenade Launcher to propel the grenade to a point up to 1,000 feet away. The grenade explodes at that point, creating a particular effect in a 20-foot-radius [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/sphere-area-of-effect-xphb\|Sphere]].

## Fragmentation Grenade

Each creature in the [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/sphere-area-of-effect-xphb\|Sphere]] makes a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 17 (`5d6`) Piercing damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Smoke Grenade

The area of the [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/sphere-area-of-effect-xphb\|Sphere]] is [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/heavily-obscured-xphb\|Heavily Obscured]] by smoke for 1 minute. A strong wind (such as the [[Rules/Source/spells/gust-of-wind-xphb\|Gust of Wind]] spell) disperses the smoke.

## Gunpowder

Setting fire to a container full of Gunpowder causes it to explode. When a container explodes, each creature in a 10-foot-radius [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/sphere-area-of-effect-xphb\|Sphere]] centered on the container makes a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10 (`3d6`) Fire damage (for a powder horn) or 24 (`7d6`) Fire damage (for a keg) on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
