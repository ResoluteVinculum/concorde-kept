# Cloak of Invisibility
*Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement)*  



This cloak has 3 charges and regains `1d3` expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the cloak, you can take a [[Rules/Source/actions#Magic\|Magic]] action to pull its hood over your head and expend 1 charge to give yourself the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Invisible\|Invisible]] condition for 1 hour. The effect ends early if you pull the hood down (no action required) or cease wearing the cloak.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 244. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span>*
