# Cloak of the Manta Ray
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![/Rules/Source/items/img/cloak-of-the-manta-ray.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/items/img/cloak-of-the-manta-ray.webp)


While wearing this cloak, you can breathe underwater, and you have a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/swim-speed-xphb\|Swim Speed]] of 60 feet.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 245. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
