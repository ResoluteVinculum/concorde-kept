# Brooch of Shielding
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![/Rules/Source/items/img/brooch-of-shielding.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/items/img/brooch-of-shielding.webp)


While wearing this brooch, you have [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/resistance-xphb\|Resistance]] to Force damage, and you have [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/immunity-xphb\|Immunity]] to damage from the [[Rules/Source/spells/magic-missile-xphb\|Magic Missile]] spell.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 241. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
