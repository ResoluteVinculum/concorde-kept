# Bracers of Archery
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![/Rules/Source/items/img/bracers-of-archery.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/items/img/bracers-of-archery.webp)


While wearing these bracers, you have proficiency with the [[Rules/Source/items/longbow-xphb\|Longbow]] and [[Rules/Source/items/shortbow-xphb\|Shortbow]], and you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls made with such weapons.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 240. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
