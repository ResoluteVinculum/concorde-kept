# Cloak of Billowing
*Wondrous item, common*  
![/Rules/Source/items/img/cloak-of-billowing.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/items/img/cloak-of-billowing.webp)


While wearing this cloak, you can take a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb\|Bonus Action]] to make it billow dramatically for 1 minute.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 244*
