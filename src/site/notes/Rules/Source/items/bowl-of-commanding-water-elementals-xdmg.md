# Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals
*Wondrous item, rare*  
![/Rules/Source/items/img/bowl-of-commanding-water-elementals.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/items/img/bowl-of-commanding-water-elementals.webp)


While this bowl is filled with water and you are within 5 feet of it, you can take a [[Rules/Source/actions#Magic\|Magic]] action to summon a [[Rules/Source/bestiary/elemental/water-elemental-xmm\|Water Elemental]]. The elemental appears in an unoccupied space as close to the bowl as possible, understands your languages, obeys your commands, and takes its turn immediately after you on your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/initiative-xphb\|Initiative]] count. The elemental disappears after 1 hour, when it dies, or when you dismiss it as a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb\|Bonus Action]]. The bowl can't be used this way again until the next dawn.

The bowl is about 1 foot in diameter and half as deep. It holds about 3 gallons.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 240. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
