# Cloak of Many Fashions
*Wondrous item, common*  
![/Rules/Source/items/img/cloak-of-many-fashions.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/items/img/cloak-of-many-fashions.webp)


While wearing this cloak, you can take a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb\|Bonus Action]] to change the style, color, and apparent quality of the garment. The cloak's weight doesn't change. Regardless of its appearance, the cloak can't be anything but a cloak. Although it can duplicate the appearance of other magic cloaks, it doesn't gain their magical properties.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 245*
