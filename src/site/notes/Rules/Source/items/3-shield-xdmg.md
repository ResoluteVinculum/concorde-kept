# +3 Shield
*Armor ([[Rules/Source/items/shield-xphb\|shield]]), very rare*

While holding this Shield, you have a +3 bonus to [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/armor-class-xphb\|Armor Class]], in addition to the Shield's normal bonus to AC.
