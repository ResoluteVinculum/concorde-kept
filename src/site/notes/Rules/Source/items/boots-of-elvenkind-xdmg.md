# Boots of Elvenkind
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  
![/Rules/Source/items/img/boots-of-elvenkind.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/items/img/boots-of-elvenkind.webp)


While you wear these boots, your steps make no sound, regardless of the surface you are moving across. You also have [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/advantage-xphb\|Advantage]] on Dexterity ([[Rules/Source/skills#Stealth\|Stealth]]) checks.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 239. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
