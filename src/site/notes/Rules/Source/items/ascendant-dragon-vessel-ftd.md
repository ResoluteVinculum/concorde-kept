# Ascendant Dragon Vessel
*Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement)*  



This vessel can be a potion bottle, drinking horn, or other container meant to hold a liquid.

As a bonus action, if the vessel is empty, you can speak the command word to fill the vessel with one of the following (your choice): ale, olive oil, mead, wine, whiskey, a [[Rules/Source/items/potion-of-healing-xdmg\|potion of healing]], a [[Rules/Source/items/potion-of-greater-healing-xdmg\|potion of greater healing]], a [[Rules/Source/items/potion-of-superior-healing-xdmg\|potion of superior healing]], a [[Rules/Source/items/potion-of-supreme-healing-xdmg\|potion of supreme healing]], a [[Rules/Source/items/potion-of-climbing-xdmg\|potion of climbing]], a [[Rules/Source/items/potion-of-fire-breath-xdmg\|potion of fire breath]], a [[Rules/Source/items/potion-of-flying-xdmg\|potion of flying]], or a [[Rules/Source/items/potion-of-dragons-majesty-ftd\|potion of dragon's majesty]]. Once this property is used, it can't be used until the next dawn. A potion you create in this way loses its magical properties if it isn't imbibed within 24 hours.

*Source: Fizban's Treasury of Dragons p. 27*
