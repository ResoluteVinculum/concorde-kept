# Chain
*Adventuring gear*  


- **Cost**: 5 gp
- **Weight**: 10.0 lbs.

As a [[Rules/Source/actions#Utilize\|Utilize]] action, you can wrap a Chain around an unwilling creature within 5 feet of yourself that has the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Grappled\|Grappled]], [[Rules/Source/conditions#Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]], or [[Rules/Source/conditions#Restrained\|Restrained]] condition if you succeed on a DC 13 Strength ([[Rules/Source/skills#Athletics\|Athletics]]) check. If the creature's legs are bound, the creature has the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Restrained\|Restrained]] condition until it escapes. Escaping the Chain requires the creature to make a successful DC 18 Dexterity ([[Rules/Source/skills#Acrobatics\|Acrobatics]]) check as an action. Bursting the Chain requires a successful DC 20 Strength ([[Rules/Source/skills#Athletics\|Athletics]]) check as an action.

*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 224. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
