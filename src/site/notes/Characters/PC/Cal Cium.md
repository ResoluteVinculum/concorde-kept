# Cursed Cacophony
Blessed with the gift of gab, Cal lived his live orating and performing [unique musical performances](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9P8C6-XqaNs&t=66s). Sometimes acrobatic and sometimes straight up preposterous, Cal aimed to entertain. All the way up until an extreme stunt where he played Flight of the Bumblebee on the violin in a swarm of bees: little did he know he was allergic.
