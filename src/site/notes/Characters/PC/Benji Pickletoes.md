# Moggy Debris
Benji Pickletoes' military prowess led to the devastation of two states at the mercy of his blade. At the end of the last battle, Benji was left alone amongst the carnage he wrought. Listening to nothing but the yowling wind... Wait, *yowling?*

In the wreckage of the battle, wielding a shield and brandishing makeshift armor:  a kitten. Benji took [[Pickles\|Pickles]] into his care and beginning an adventurer's life. 

# Schrödinger's Sourdough 
Battling the climate, the terrain, and monsters through the years, Benji and Pickles forged an unbreakable bond. The [[Sourdough Syndicate\|Sourdough Syndicate]] kidnapped Pickles, ransacking Benji's cottage in the process. They demand payment with no promise of safe return.
