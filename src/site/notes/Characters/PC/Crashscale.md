# Spiral Spirituality
The Spire serves two primary purposes, resource extraction and isolation. On the western side of the island, Crashscale trained under his master, [[Sunderflash\|Sunderflash]]. Those seeking the bounty of The Spire brought destruction to the Monastery of the Seal. Driving Crashscale into self isolation.
