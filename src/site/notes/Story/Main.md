# Flavor
> [!Note] Setting
> Throw it in either high fantasy or dark fantasy where they are working for a corrupt aristocrat.
> - Allows for complex decisions
> - Allows me to ask for immoral behavior and for them to face it
> - Probably won't stay along O Brother Where Art Thou, should replace Soggy Bottom Boys with something else
> - Who they think is the BBEG initially should end up becoming their ally as they learn their Boss is EVIL
> - Nix that, no BBEG until solidified group, keep the stakes on par with level. Level 1 to 12 if possible, don't raise the stakes until the group stabilizes.
> - Players will need some reason their character is working for this aristocrat: sellsword, debt, love of the game, etc.

# Outline
- [[Story/Main#Act 0 - Meet and Greet\|#Act 0 - Meet and Greet]]: Level 1; Social Encounter for getting the job, unique to each player and a group combat encounter.
	- You are sent to protect a trade wagon travelling from [[Story/Setting/Tallywag\|Tallywag]] to [[Story/Setting/Castle Embrea\|Castle Embrea]]
- [[Story/Main#Act 1 - Transport\|#Act 1 - Transport]]: Level 1 - 3; Assigned tasks in increasing moral complexity
	- Fetch - Your employer sends you on a standard trade route and asks you to fetch something along the way. That something being a mysterious package that has been left at the [[Story/Setting/Layell Citadel\|Layell Citadel]] by a retreival team. `DC 11 History` for any character knowledgeable of the organization to recognize the history of the Duke using the citadel to launder. Contained in this package is the [[Rules/Source/items/baba-yagas-mortar-and-pestle-tce\|Baba Yaga's Mortar and Pestle]], it is magically disguised. When a player holds it, they only notice the negative effect. The package also contains a blank document, [[Rules/Bespoke/Items/Enduring Expose\|Enduring Expose]]. `DC 25 Arcana` for any character to recognize either of these objects.
	

# Act 0 - Meet and Grunt
Level 1, groups of two classes get a social interaction for how they got their "job", then some time can pass and the group gets assigned to some combat encounter as part of their job (probably goblins in a cave).
